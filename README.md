# The Last Night at Changi

A puzzle story in eight rooms, set on the final night before Changi Hospital closed in 1997.

**Play:** https://fang89.github.io/last-night-at-changi/

You are Nurse Ellis, three months qualified, left to sit the last night with the building. Each room is a case note with a puzzle: logic, ciphers, a letter lock, a pager, a floor of tiles, a drug cabinet, a theatre clock. Work each one by hand or type its answer to unlock the next room. Things you find go into your pockets and can be dragged onto what you find later. Every answer has one shaded letter on the answer sheet, and you need all of them before morning.

Hints are available in every room. Answers are checked in the browser against encrypted locks, so reading the page source will not spoil them. Progress is saved in your browser.

Works on desktop and phone, in light and dark mode. One self-contained HTML file.

A work of fiction inspired by the stories told about Old Changi Hospital. The real buildings are closed and fenced off; please enjoy them from here.
