Changi Hospital · Final night before closure · 1997

The Last Night at Changi

A puzzle story in eight rooms. Work each puzzle by hand, or type the answer, to unlock the next room. What you carry in your pockets can be used on what you find. Every answer has one shaded letter on your answer sheet, and you will need all of them before morning.
